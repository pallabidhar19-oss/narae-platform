🇰🇷 Korea Export Side

What Korea checks on exit

  • HS classification under Korea's Schedule B export codes
  • Export licence requirement (none for standard merchandise)
  • Strategic goods / dual-use screening (lightsticks with batteries: flag for lithium battery export rules)
  • Certificate of Origin — required for Japan FTA preferential rate
  • IP licence documentation — Korea Customs Service enforces outbound IP too
  • Packaging labelling in Korean — must match export invoice
🇯🇵 Japan Import Side

What Japan checks on entry

  • Japan Tariff Schedule classification (HS-based, 9-digit)
  • JKFTA preferential duty rate — requires valid Korean Certificate of Origin
  • PSE mark requirement — mandatory for certain electronic goods (lightsticks with charging circuits)
  • Japanese labelling law — country of origin + importer name in Japanese on consumer goods
  • Food/cosmetics prohibition — no skincare or food items without separate MHLW approval
  • Consumption tax (JCT) — 10% on imports above ¥10,000 declared value

03 — Corridor flags for this shipment
🔴 PSE Mark — Lightstick (USB-C rechargeable) Japan's Product Safety Electrical Appliance & Materials (PSE) law requires a diamond PSE mark on rechargeable electronic products sold in Japan. This must be obtained before the product enters Japan — it cannot be resolved at customs. Lead time: 4-8 weeks. Route: apply through a Japan-registered importer or use a JQA-accredited testing body in Korea.
⚠️ Japanese labelling — Hoodie & Lightstick Japan's Act against Unjustifiable Premiums and Misleading Representations requires country-of-origin and importer name in Japanese on consumer goods. Must be on the product or packaging before import. Applies to both items. Estimated cost: ¥80-150 per unit for sticker labelling run.
⚠️ Certificate of Origin — all items JKFTA 0% preferential rate requires a valid Korean Certificate of Origin issued by the Korea Chamber of Commerce. Without it, standard Japan MFN duty rates apply (e.g. 10.9% on cotton apparel). Apply at least 5 business days before shipment.
✅ No dual-use / export control issues Standard K-entertainment merchandise (apparel, printed goods, accessories) does not trigger Korean strategic goods export controls. Lightstick lithium battery: below the threshold that triggers Korea's battery export licence requirement at this shipment volume.
04 — What this layer adds vs checking one side at a time
Checking Korea only

What you miss

  • PSE requirement invisible — only shows up at Narita
  • Japanese labelling requirement invisible until arrival
  • JCT calculation not visible until Japanese customs bill arrives
Checking Japan only

What you miss

  • Certificate of Origin not prepared — lose JKFTA 0% rate
  • Korea export invoice format mismatch — Japan customs rejects
  • Korean IP documentation not prepared for export
