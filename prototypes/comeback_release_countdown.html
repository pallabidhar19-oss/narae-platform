04 — What the SEVENTEEN HAPPY BURSTDAY case showed
🔴 The slip started at production — it became a story at the venue SEVENTEEN's 2025 US tour merch arrived after fans had already attended shows — the delay was publicly documented across fan communities and entertainment media. The root cause traced back to production and logistics timing, not a single customs hold. By the time the delay was visible to operations, there was no recovery option.
⚠️ A 7-day production slip compounds through every downstream step Production delay → QC pushed → freight departure pushed → customs clearance pushed → fulfilment pushed. Each handoff is invisible to the next team unless there's a shared timeline. The freight team doesn't know production slipped until they're asked for a truck that isn't coming.
✅ What this layer adds The same approach as every other Narae module: aggregate what's already tracked — production schedules, freight bookings, customs timelines — into one view, held against the one date that can't move. A 7-day production slip shows up here as a shipping risk on day 1, not when the truck doesn't arrive.
