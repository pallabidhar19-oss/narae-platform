Trainee Investment Ledger

Every agency already runs monthly evaluations and already tracks training spend. What none of them currently see in one place is whether the two are converging — years and cost heading toward a debut-ready artist — or quietly diverging into a write-off nobody flagged until the contract came up for renewal.

Today

Two ledgers that never meet

  • A&R runs the monthly evaluation and scores each trainee on vocal, dance, and stage presence — that record lives with A&R.
  • Finance tracks lessons, housing, styling, and coaching costs per trainee — that record lives with finance.
  • A trainee whose cost is climbing while their score has quietly plateaued for three straight evaluations looks fine in either ledger alone. It only looks like a problem once someone lines the two up — and usually that's at contract-renewal time, not before.

With this layer

The same two inputs, one trajectory

  • No change to how evaluations are run or scored — this only pairs the existing monthly score with the existing cost-to-date, automatically, per trainee.
  • Trajectories that are accelerating relative to spend surface as early candidates for fast-tracking — this cuts both ways, not just toward cutting people.
  • Trajectories heading toward a write-off surface months before the next renewal decision, instead of at it.
